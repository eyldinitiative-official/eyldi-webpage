Latest Research · Leaders Developing Leaders

Developing ECE leaders: research on what works.

EYLDI interviewed 17 early childhood leaders from 7 U.S. states to find out how they were developing rising leaders and what made them most effective. Many leaders are already doing this work. This research can help you learn how to do it even more effectively.

Why This Matters

Great leadership doesn't just happen. Someone develops it.

EYLDI's first study, in 2021, found that ECE leaders, especially those from marginalized backgrounds, often lacked access to developmental relationships with more experienced leaders. The latest research picks up from there, asking what those relationships actually look like when they work well to develop rising ECE leaders. Read about the 2021 study →

A Leader-Developer invests

An experienced ECE leader builds a real developmental relationship with a rising leader by guiding, understanding, and enabling their growth.

→

A new leader grows

That rising leader becomes more confident and effective, and more representative of the communities ECE serves.

→

Programs strengthen

Better-led programs mean stronger teams, higher educator retention, and more consistent, higher-quality care.

→

Children benefit

Because leadership quality is linked to program quality, children and families ultimately feel the difference.
