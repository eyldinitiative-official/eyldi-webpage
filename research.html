Leaders Developing Leaders

Two studies, one throughline. In 2021, EYLDI surveyed ~500 Early Childhood and Education (ECE) professionals and found that leaders — especially from marginalized backgrounds — lacked access to developmental relationships. In 2025/26, EYLDI interviewed 17 ECE leaders who supported the growth of a rising leader, revealing five competencies that predict effective leadership development.

Two studies, one throughline

2021 · Diversify Early Childhood Leadership

EYLDI's founding study — a national survey asking what's holding ECE leaders back.

  • ~500 ECE professionals surveyed (457 analyzed)
  • 27 companion interviews
  • 82% endorsed advancing leaders from marginalized backgrounds
  • 71% said the sector has too few leaders with diverse backgrounds
  • Key finding: leaders — especially from marginalized backgrounds — lacked access to developmental relationships with more experienced leaders
Download the 2021 Report (PDF) →

2025/26 · Leaders Developing Leaders

EYLDI's current, most complete study — in-depth interviews asking what actually works.

  • 17 in-depth Leader-Developer interviews
  • Behavioral Event Interview protocol
  • 7 U.S. states, March–July 2024
  • Key finding: five competencies predict effective leadership development
