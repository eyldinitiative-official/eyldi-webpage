Five behaviors for effective leader development

Competencies are the characteristics that predict outstanding impact in a given situation. Some competencies are easily acquired, such as skills and knowledge, while others are deeper, such as traits (e.g. patience), values (e.g. the belief that everyone has the capacity to grow), and motives (e.g. getting satisfaction from helping others grow).

Below are the five competencies we found linked with effective leader development. The competencies are grouped below based on our research: one foundational competency every leader showed, three competencies that worked together to drive strong outcomes in developmental relationships, and one rarer competency that most strongly predicted excellent development outcomes.

The Data

Who we studied, and what we found

Leader development is already happening in ECE through developmental relationships – relationships in which a more experienced leader (we call them Leader-Developers) helps a rising leader (Leader-Learners) to develop skills, navigate career choices, and become a stronger leader themselves. In this study, we spoke with Leader-Developers.

88%

Leader-Learners in the study were female

62%

Leader-Learners were Black, Latina/o, Asian & multiracial
